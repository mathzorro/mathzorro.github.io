About

Christopher R. H. Hanusa is a professor in the Department of Mathematics at Queens College (CUNY), where he has been on the faculty since 2008.

Christopher R. H. Hanusa is a Professor of Mathematics at Queens College of the City University of New York. His research is in algebraic and enumerative combinatorics. Specifically, he studies the combinatorics of flow polytopes, lattice point enumeration, and combinatorial interpretations of Coxeter groups.

Professor Hanusa is also interested in the visualization and artistic qualities of mathematical concepts and aims to popularize mathematics to a wider audience through his mathematical artwork. Visit Christopher Hanusa on the web at hanusa.xyz and on social media at @mathzorro.

Christopher R. H. Hanusa is a mathematical artist and a professor of mathematics at Queens College of the City University of New York. He uses computational software to design artwork inspired by the inherent beauty of mathematics. He pushes boundaries of skills and knowledge to learn something new in each piece.

As with his teaching, he strives for audience participation and engagement. The final pieces aim to inspire a curiosity about the mathematical nugget underlying the visualization. Visit Christopher Hanusa's portfolio on the web at christopherhanusa.com and follow him on social media at @mathzorro.

Christopher R. H. Hanusa is a Professor of Mathematics at Queens College of the City University of New York. He served as the College's first Faculty Liaison for Evaluation and Assessment, leading in matters relating to assessment and accreditation.

A skilled and empathetic facilitator, he coordinates conversations on the committees he chairs and also between faculty and the college administration. His administrative philosophy centers on building sustainable systems that allow faculty and students to thrive. Visit Christopher Hanusa on the web at hanusa.xyz and on LinkedIn.

Office Hours
