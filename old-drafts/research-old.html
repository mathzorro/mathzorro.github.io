Research

Explaining the universe through Discrete Geometry and Combinatorics.

My research focuses on Algebraic and Enumerative Combinatorics. I utilize experimental mathematics—using computers to generate data, visualize structures, and formulate conjectures—to explore the properties of geometric objects.

Core Research Interests

  • Flow Polytopes: Investigating the combinatorial properties and volume formulas of flow polytopes related to various graphs.
  • Coxeter Groups: Analyzing the structure of reflection groups and their associated heaps and fully commutative elements.
  • Lattice Point Enumeration: Counting integer points in polyhedra to solve problems in representation theory and number theory.
  • Perfect Matchings: Studying dimer models and tiling problems on graphs.

My work is regularly published in journals such as the Journal of Algebraic Combinatorics and Discrete Mathematics.
