Empowering Creativity

I believe that mathematics is a creative endeavor. My teaching bridges the gap between abstract theory and tangible creation.

Mathematical Design Course

This flagship course invites students—often from non-STEM backgrounds—to use code and geometry to create physical art. It demystifies mathematics by turning it into a tool for expression.

  • Methodology: The "Double Diamond" design process (Discover, Define, Develop, Deliver).
  • Tech Stack: Desmos, Mathematica, OpenSCAD.

Digital Fabrication

I integrate modern manufacturing into the curriculum. Students learn not just to solve equations, but to print them. We utilize 3D printers for topological surfaces and laser cutters for tessellation projects.

  • Outcome: Students gain hard skills in rapid prototyping and CAD.
  • Equipment: Prusa 3D Printers, Glowforge Laser Cutters, Axidraw Plotters.

Mentorship & Research

I am dedicated to guiding students through their own research journeys. I have directed over two dozen undergraduate and graduate thesis projects, helping students transition into PhD programs and technical industry roles.
