Research & Discovery

Exploring the intersection of algebraic combinatorics, visualization, and discrete geometry.

Flow Polytopes

My work investigates the combinatorial properties of flow polytopes, specifically exploring their volume formulas and connections to the Kostant partition function. By applying discrete geometry, I aim to uncover the structural rules that govern these complex shapes.

  • Focus: Lidskii formulas and volume calculations.
  • Application: Representation theory and network flow analysis.

Coxeter Groups

I study the combinatorics of Coxeter groups, focusing on fully commutative elements and heap structures. This research bridges abstract algebra with visual representation, allowing us to "see" algebraic properties through geometric interpretation.

  • Focus: Enumeration of fully commutative elements.
  • Key Concept: Heap of pieces and diagrammatic algebra.

Experimental Mathematics

I believe in using the computer as a laboratory. By generating vast amounts of combinatorial data and visualizing the results, we can formulate new conjectures that would be invisible to the naked eye. This computational approach drives my work in both pure math and generative art.

  • Tools: SageMath, Python, and Wolfram Language.
  • Outcome: Data-driven conjectures and computer-assisted proofs.
