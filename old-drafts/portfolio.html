Art from Algorithms

Hanusa Design explores the aesthetic possibilities of mathematical rules, transforming code into physical objects and visual experiences.

Generative Jewelry

My jewelry collection brings abstract topology to life. Using nylon and steel 3D printing, I create wearable sculptures based on Möbius strips, torus knots, and parametric curves. Each piece is generated via code, ensuring geometric precision.

  • Featured At: National Museum of Mathematics (MoMath).
  • Materials: Selective Laser Sintered Nylon, Gold-plated Steel.

Plotters & Prints

Using AxiDraw pen plotters and high-resolution digital printing, I explore the repetition and variation inherent in generative systems. Works like "Riemannscapes" visualize complex functions, while "Padovan Mountains" explore recursive sequences.

  • Tools: Wolfram Language, Processing, AxiDraw.
  • Concept: Controlled randomness and emergent complexity.

Exhibitions

My work has been featured in galleries that celebrate the intersection of art and mathematics. I am a regular contributor to the Bridges Organization art exhibitions and the Joint Mathematics Meetings.

  • Recent Shows: Bridges Conference (Math + Fashion), JMM Art Gallery.
