Mathematical Design

I developed this course to bring the beauty and creativity of mathematics to general education students. Students learn about mathematical functions as tools to design works of art on the online graphing calculator Desmos. They translate this digital artwork onto paper through the use of an AxiDraw pen plotter.

Mathematical Computing

Students learn the basics of programming in Wolfram Mathematica and apply their skills to design mathematical sculpture and create an interactive app. This course aims to give students a mathematical research experience involving creativity, pushing boundaries, and productive failure.
